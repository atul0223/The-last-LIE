# The Last Lie — Playtest Facilitator Guide

> **Role**: Facilitator / Test Observer  
> **Target Audience**: 10 external playtesters (varied experience with deduction, mystery, and logic games)  
> **Objective**: Measure onboarding friction, evidence board comprehension, deduction fairness, and retention pull without biasing player decisions.

---

## 1. Principles & Protocol

1. **Strict Neutrality**: Never give hints, confirm hypotheses, or explain clues during active play. If a player asks "Is this right?" or "What does this mean?", respond with:
   - *"What does it suggest to you?"*
   - *"How would you interpret that in light of what you've found?"*
2. **Think-Aloud Protocol**: Encourage the tester to verbalize their thoughts continuously.
   - If silent for >20 seconds, prompt: *"Tell me what you're thinking right now."* or *"What are you looking for?"*
3. **No Interventions on Friction**: When a player struggles with UI elements (e.g., drawing board links, unlocking clues, submitting theories), observe and record the exact friction point. Do not step in unless the player is completely deadlocked for >2 minutes.

---

## 2. Session Flow (Approx. 45–60 Minutes)

### Phase 1: Welcome & Setup (5 min)
1. Welcome tester, offer water, verify device comfort (tester phone or lab phone).
2. Present `docs/playtest/consent-note.md` and obtain signature/verbal agreement.
3. Introduce the think-aloud protocol:
   > *"Today we're testing the game, not you. There are no right or wrong ways to navigate. Please speak your thoughts out loud as you play — tell us what looks interesting, what confuses you, and what you plan to do next. We will stay quiet to watch how the app communicates on its own."*

### Phase 2: First Case Playthrough — `case_0001` (15–20 min)
1. Launch app to Home screen.
2. Note time to start case.
3. Observe Briefing reading speed, transition to Evidence tab.
4. Record clue unlocks (default vs unlocked hints).
5. Watch Evidence Board usage:
   - Do they arrange cards?
   - Do they draw links between cards?
   - Do they understand contradiction links?
6. Watch Theory formulation and submission.
7. Observe Reveal screen reaction:
   - Do they read the timeline explanation?
   - Do they check valid vs invalid links?
   - Did the reveal feel fair or arbitrary?

### Phase 3: Natural Continuation Test (5 min)
1. Remain silent after the Reveal screen.
2. Observe whether the tester:
   - Returns to Home/Library voluntarily.
   - Specifically asks: *"Can I do another case?"*
   - Stops and waits for instruction.

### Phase 4: Second Case Playthrough — `case_0002` or `case_0003` (15 min)
1. If the tester continues voluntarily, observe progression, speed improvements, and retention.

### Phase 5: Survey & Debrief (10 min)
1. Hand the tester `docs/playtest/post-session-survey.md`.
2. Conduct brief open-ended debrief:
   - *"What was the single most satisfying moment?"*
   - *"What was the most frustrating or confusing interaction?"*
   - *"Did you feel the solution made logical sense based on the clues?"*
3. Thank tester, log results, and archive observation notes.
