# Participant Information & Consent Form

**Project**: *The Last Lie* Mobile Game Playtest  
**Session Length**: Approximately 45–60 minutes  
**Device**: Mobile device (iOS or Android)  

---

## Purpose of this Session
You are invited to participate in a research playtest for *The Last Lie*, an episodic deduction and logic game. Our primary goal is to evaluate user experience, narrative clarity, interface intuitiveness, and game difficulty. **We are testing the game, not your abilities.**

## What You Will Do
1. Play through one or more short mystery cases on a mobile phone.
2. Speak your thoughts aloud as you navigate clues, organize an evidence board, and construct case theories.
3. Complete a short 6-question survey and participate in a brief conversational debrief.

## Confidentiality & Data Protection
- Any notes, screen recordings, or audio taken during this session are used strictly for internal development and UX improvements.
- No personally identifiable information (PII) will ever be published, sold, or shared with third parties.
- All session records are tagged with an anonymous Participant ID (e.g., `P-01`).

## Voluntary Participation
Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may ask to pause, take a break, or discontinue the session at any time without providing a reason.
