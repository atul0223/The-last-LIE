THE LAST LIE: Infinite is an analytical murder mystery and corporate intrigue investigation game. The core mechanics center on reading witness statements, examining access logs, reconstructing timelines, identifying logical contradictions, and submitting deductive theories.

- **Zero Graphic Gore:** Crime scenes are described textually or represented diagrammatically. No decapitations, visceral gore, dismemberment, or mutilation.
