## 3. Full Description (Google Play & Apple App Store)

**NO GUESSWORK. NO LUCK. ONLY PURE DEDUCTION.**

Welcome to **THE LAST LIE: Infinite**, an analytical detective game where every mystery is completely solvable through pure logical deduction. In a world steeped in corporate espionage, hidden identities, and secret cabals, only one suspect is lying. Your mission: find the contradiction that shatters their alibi.

### 🔎 FEATURES

- **AN INTERACTIVE EVIDENCE BOARD**
  Drag, arrange, and link evidence cards on a pannable crime board. Connect suspect statements against access logs, forensic timestamps, and physical objects to expose fatal contradictions.

- **FAIR-PLAY MYSTERY MECHANICS**
  Every case adheres to classic golden-age detective fair-play rules. You will never be cheated by hidden information, arbitrary intuition, or unsolvable leaps. If an alibi is false, the physical proof is in your hands.

- **INFINITE PROCEDURAL INVESTIGATIONS**
  Powered by an advanced combinatorial mystery generator, solve over 120 unique procedural cases ranging from swift 5-minute inquiries to intricate multi-suspect master investigations.

- **THE QUIET: CANONICAL STORYLINE**
  Unravel the overarching shadow network behind the city. Pin clues from canonical story chapters to your persistent Conspiracy Map, revealing hidden links between recurring characters, corporate syndicates, and classified operations.

- **DAILY ROTATIONS & COMMUNITY WEEKLY CASES**
  Tackle a new daily investigation every 24 hours to maintain your solver streak. Compete in weekly high-stakes cases to compare your deduction accuracy, clue unlock efficiency, and theory scores against detectives worldwide.

- **FRIEND CHALLENGES & SPOILER-FREE SHARING**
  Challenge friends to crack the same mystery seed. Share beautiful, spoiler-free detective dossier cards that compare hypotheses, deduction speed, and score breakdowns without ruining the mystery for others.

- **OFFLINE-FIRST INVESTIGATION**
  Solve on the subway, in flight, or off the grid. Offline starter investigations require zero network connection.
