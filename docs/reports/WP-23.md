# WP-23 Report: Progress Tab

## Summary
- **Authoritative Engine Progression**: Implemented pure progression derivation using `@lastlie/case-engine` (`applyResult`, `rankFor`, `nextRankRequirements`). The UI strictly delegates rank calculation and mastery gate evaluations to the engine.
- **Rank Card & Mastery Roadmap**: Displays player detective rank (`Trainee` through `Master Detective`), level indicator, animated XP progress bar, next rank targets, and detailed mastery clearance gate progress (e.g. required qualifying solves at difficulty thresholds with ≥60% evidence quality).
- **Case Archive with Theory Comparisons**: Archive list of solved cases showing difficulty, solve dates, score breakdowns, star ratings, and clear status badges (`✓ SOLVED` / `✗ FAILED`). For replayed cases, shows side-by-side or stacked Theory Comparison contrasting the initial attempt with the replay resolution.
- **Interactive Replay Initiation**: Replay CTA initiates a fresh session with `mode: 'replay'`, receives 0.25× replay XP scaling per SYSTEM_DESIGN §7.3, and navigates immediately into the case session.
- **Suspect Dossiers (Solved Cases Only)**: Character dossier cards displaying monogram avatar, name, role, public description, and case appearances. Unsolved case characters are strictly guarded and excluded from dossiers.

## Acceptance Checklist
- ✅ **Rank card**: Displays current rank, level badge, XP bar, next rank XP delta, and mastery gate solves requirements (`progress.rank.card`, `progress.rank.gate`).
- ✅ **Authoritative engine ranks**: Uses `rankFor` and `nextRankRequirements` from `@lastlie/case-engine`; no ranks are computed locally.
- ✅ **Case archive**: Renders solved cases from stored progress with score, date, rating stars, and correct/incorrect indicators (`progress.archive.list`, `progress.archive.item.<caseId>`).
- ✅ **Replay session flow**: Initiates `startSession({ caseId, mode: 'replay' })` and routes to the new session on press (`progress.archive.replay.<caseId>`).
- ✅ **Previous vs Replay Theory comparison**: Shows initial attempt hypothesis/score next to replay resolution (`progress.archive.comparison.<caseId>`).
- ✅ **Dossiers from solved cases only**: Only characters from solved cases are included in the dossier list; characters from unplayed or in-progress cases are completely excluded (`progress.dossier.character.<id>`).
- ✅ **Accessibility & Safe Test Execution**: Touchables have `accessibilityLabel` and `accessibilityRole`, fonts support 200% scaling, and color is never the sole indicator.
